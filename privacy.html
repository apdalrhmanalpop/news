Privacy

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. We do not get your Google password. We store your email, your chosen countries and language, and the time you signed up, only to remember your choices. You can delete this data at any time with "Delete my data" in "My news".

We count visits with a random device ID stored in your browser (no name or email attached) to see how many people use the site. We do not sell your data. Translation uses Google Translate in your browser. Ads, if any appear later, may set their own cookies.

Articles are short pieces written with AI from public headlines and summaries of the cited outlets. Always check the original source.
