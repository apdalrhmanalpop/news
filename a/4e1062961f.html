Nepali Employee Killed in Houthi Strike on Saudi Refinery

An assault by Houthi forces targeting a petroleum facility in Jazan, Saudi Arabia, has left one Nepali laborer dead and multiple people injured. Following the incident, the Ministry of Energy temporarily halted certain activities at the site to evaluate the extent of the harm.

In response to the escalation, the Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen announced that coalition forces located and demolished the ballistic missile launcher responsible for the strike in Hodeidah.
