Coalition Neutralizes Houthi Military Assets and Missile Launcher in Yemen

The Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen executed targeted military strikes aimed at degrading the capabilities of the Houthi militia. Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki, the spokesperson for the Coalition, reported that the precise operation successfully dismantled fifty legitimate military targets.

According to Al-Maliki, these operations took place across the governorates of Sanaa, Hodeidah, and Saada. Additionally, Coalition forces succeeded in destroying a ballistic missile launcher during the campaign.
