German courts overwhelmed by wave of erroneous AI-generated lawsuits

Judicial institutions across Germany are currently facing a massive surge of legal actions produced by artificial intelligence. These filings are frequently packed with mistakes and extend to excessive lengths, creating a heavy burden for the court system.

To address this unprecedented volume, certain state justice ministers are considering the implementation of a new charge to slow down the filings. However, this potential measure introduces distinct challenges of its own.
