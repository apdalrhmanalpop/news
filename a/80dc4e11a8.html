CSIR Opens Recruitment for Applicants Ranging from 10th Pass to Graduates

The Council Of Scientific And Industrial Research has announced a recruitment drive welcoming applicants with educational qualifications spanning from the 10th pass level up to university graduates.

Individuals wishing to apply are strongly urged by authorities to submit their documentation early, rather than waiting until the final deadline. Furthermore, officials have clarified that no appeals for extending the submission period will be accepted under any circumstances.
