Driver Held Over Cairo Ring Road Tanker Fire

Egyptian authorities have placed the driver responsible for a fiery fuel tanker crash on the Cairo Ring Road in Warraq into custody. The incident occurred on Wednesday when the vehicle flipped over and burst into flames.

During interrogation by the Public Prosecution, the suspect acknowledged operating the vehicle alongside his father. Meanwhile, Prime Minister Mostafa Madbouly went to the hospital to check on individuals injured in the accident.
