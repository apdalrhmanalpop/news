Schools in England Face New Guidance to Boost Daily Reading Time

Educational institutions are set to receive instructions from the government to dedicate specific periods for children to read for enjoyment. This measure forms part of an upcoming curriculum overhaul designed to tackle falling literacy levels among pupils.

Officials indicate that the initiative also aims to counter the negative impacts of excessive digital device usage, which authorities describe as mindless scrolling.
