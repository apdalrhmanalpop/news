TMG and Tamer Hosny Delay Madinaty Concert Over Ring Road Tragedy

Talaat Moustafa Group alongside Egyptian vocalist Tamer Hosny have decided to push back their upcoming performance in Madinaty. The event, initially set for November 27, will now take place on December 4. Organizers stated the delay is a gesture of solidarity with the relatives of those who died in a severe accident on the Ring Road.

Individuals who already secured tickets for the original date can still use them for the rescheduled December concert, as current passes remain fully valid.
