Main Suspect in Massive Chhattisgarh Liquor Scam Extradited From UAE

Vikas Agrawal, a primary suspect in a multi-billion rupee liquor scam in Chhattisgarh, has been returned from the United Arab Emirates. He was the subject of an active Interpol Red Notice.

Local law enforcement had registered the case regarding corruption within the state's alcohol supply network spanning the years 2019 through 2022.
