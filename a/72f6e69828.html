Saudi Arabia and Pakistan Explore Defense and Security Cooperation

Saudi Minister of Defense Prince Khalid bin Salman traveled to Islamabad to hold discussions with Pakistan's Chief of Army Staff and Chief of Defense Forces, Field Marshal Asim Munir. The high-level talks occurred during the second gathering of the Political and Defense Strategy Committee under the Makkah Alliance for Defense.

Both officials emphasized the crucial function the alliance serves in bolstering regional security and stability. Furthermore, they explored potential strategies to enhance joint defense capabilities between the two nations.
