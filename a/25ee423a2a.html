Rahul Gandhi Urges NDA Allies to Reject Delimitation Plans

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi has publicly urged the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) to reconsider its stance on the ongoing delimitation initiative, prompting national attention. Beyond the DMK, he also called upon southern partners of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)—specifically the Telugu Desam Party (TDP) headed by Chandrababu Naidu and the Janasena Party (JSP) under Pawan Kalyan—to withdraw their backing for the controversial proposal.

The prominent opposition figure directed his appeal toward these regional political factions, asking them to prioritize the nation's broader interests and drop their support for the delimitation push.
