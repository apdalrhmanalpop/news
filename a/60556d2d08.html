Jaish operative plotting air base strike captured in Gujarat

Security forces in Gujarat have taken a Jaish-e-Mohammed operative into custody for plotting a strike against a military installation. The individual, identified as Mohammad Saharul Islam and originally from Assam, intended to target an air force facility located in Vadodara.

Authorities revealed that the suspect was preparing to carry out a solitary assault on the designated military base before being intercepted and arrested.
