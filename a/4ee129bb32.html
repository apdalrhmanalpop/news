Saudi Arabia Warns UN Security Council of Terrorist Tech Exploitation

During a United Nations Security Council open debate addressing conflict resolution and rapid technological advancement, Saudi Arabia expressed serious concern regarding extremist organizations leveraging new innovations. Faris Al-Otaibi, the Acting Chargé d'Affaires of the Saudi Permanent Mission to the UN in New York, highlighted the specific threat of militant factions utilizing technological components to build military capabilities.

The warning delivered in New York emphasizes the Kingdom's vigilance regarding modern security challenges at the international level.
