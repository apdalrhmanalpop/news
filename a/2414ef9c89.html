Assam Reports Over 6,000 Child Marriage Arrests Since 2021

Himanta Sarma revealed that authorities have detained 6,705 individuals in connection with underage marriage offenses since 2021. The statistics were made public to mark the inaugural International Day for the Elimination of Child, Early and Forced Marriage.

The administration emphasized that safeguarding the well-being and prospects of young females continues to be a major focus for the state.
