DWP Services Offline Following Suspected Cyber Attack

Certain pension and benefit systems remain non-operational following what is being described as a cyber incident. A former chief technology officer for the UK suggests that hackers might be responsible for the disruption affecting the Department for Work and Pensions.

At this stage, authorities have not officially verified whether a cyberattack took place. Efforts to restore the impacted services are ongoing.
