Strobe Talbott, Former Diplomat and Time Magazine Journalist, Passes Away at 80

Strobe Talbott, who transitioned from working as a foreign policy columnist and reporter for Time to holding a high-ranking position in the State Department, has died at the age of eighty.

During his career, he functioned as the deputy secretary of state. Later, he also took on the role of president at the Brookings Institution.
