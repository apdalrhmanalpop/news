Egyptian Business Tycoons Pledge EGP 30m for Ring Road Fire Victims

Three prominent Egyptian entrepreneurs have committed a total of EGP 30m to assist the households impacted by a recent blaze on the Ring Road in Al-Warraq, according to a cabinet announcement on Thursday.

Cabinet spokesperson Counselor Mohamed Adel reported that officials received multiple support proposals from business sector representatives following the Greater Cairo tragedy.
