Badenoch Urges Conservative Candidates to Back Net-Zero Scrap

Kemi Badenoch has stated that individuals running for the Conservative Party must commit to an agreement aimed at eliminating net-zero goals. According to the Tory leader, this initiative, referred to as her "candidates covenant," is designed to strengthen public trust in the political organization.

However, the proposal has faced backlash from several former members of parliament who have voiced opposition to the plan.
