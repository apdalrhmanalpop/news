Assam Police Busts Fake ICSE Certificate Scheme Involving Thousands

Authorities in Assam have apprehended three individuals in connection with a fraudulent scheme involving counterfeit ICSE certificates that affected approximately 4,900 students. The operation came to light following a formal complaint regarding the alleged creation and illegal use of Class 10 board examination records.

Law enforcement officials initiated the crackdown under a specific case registered at the CID Police Station. The ongoing investigation aims to uncover further details regarding the production and distribution of these falsified educational documents.
