Coalition Neutralizes Houthi Drone Near Jeddah Islamic Port

Saudi Arabia's capital Riyadh witnessed an announcement from the Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen regarding an aerial attack near a major maritime facility. Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki, who serves as the coalition spokesperson, stated that forces successfully intercepted and eliminated an explosive-laden unmanned aircraft sent by the Houthi militia.

The spokesperson confirmed that the operation concluded with no harm to local residents, despite the descent of wreckage resulting from the destruction of the aircraft close to Jeddah Islamic Port.
