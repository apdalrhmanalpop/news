Strobe Talbott, Former Deputy Secretary of State and Journalist, Passes Away at 80

Strobe Talbott, who worked as a journalist for Time magazine before holding the position of deputy secretary of state during the administration of President Bill Clinton, has passed away at the age of 80 on Thursday.

In addition to his journalism and government service, Talbott also led the Brookings Institution.
