Egypt and Qatar Urge Gaza Ceasefire and Closer Economic Ties

During a meeting in Cairo on Thursday, Egyptian President Abdel Fattah Al-Sisi and Qatari Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani emphasized the urgency for all involved sides to adhere to the Gaza Strip ceasefire accord. The Egyptian presidency reported that the leaders also highlighted the necessity of securing humanitarian relief delivery and initiating early recovery and reconstruction efforts promptly.

Furthermore, the discussions between the two leaders focused on regional de-escalation alongside the enhancement of bilateral cooperation in the economic and energy sectors.
