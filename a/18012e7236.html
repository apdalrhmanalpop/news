MK Stalin Accuses Vijay Of Escalating Deepam Controversy And Threatening Harmony

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister MK Stalin has directed sharp criticism at actor-turned-politician Vijay regarding the worsening situation surrounding the Deepam controversy. Stalin argued that the escalation poses a direct threat to communal harmony in the region.

The severe rebuke from the Chief Minister primarily focused on what he characterized as a dangerous surrender of hard-fought state rights. He also condemned the shifting of temple administration into a framework mandated via the Supreme Court.
