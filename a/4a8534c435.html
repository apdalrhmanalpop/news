Canadian Supreme Court Permits Quebec's Secularism Law Provision

Canada's highest court has allowed the province of Quebec to maintain a disputed legislative tool utilized for its secularism legislation. This specific measure enabled the enactment of a statute prohibiting specific public sector employees, including law enforcement personnel and educators, from displaying religious symbols while working.

Opponents of the policy have previously characterized the restrictions as discriminatory.
