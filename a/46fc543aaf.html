Historic Victorian railway station preserved for decades put on the market

A historic Victorian railway station that has remained entirely unaltered for seven decades is currently being offered for sale. The property still contains a variety of vintage railway items inside, featuring old signal lamps alongside personal luggage.

Among the preserved artifacts left behind in the building is a suitcase, offering a glimpse into the past of the dormant site.
