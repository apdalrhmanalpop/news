Kingdom Chosen to Lead OIC Occupational Safety and Health Network

Saudi Arabia will head the Organization of Islamic Cooperation’s Occupational Safety and Health Network for a duration of two years. The National Council for Occupational Safety and Health serves as the representative for the Kingdom in this role.

Delegates from OIC member countries and specialized bodies gathered on November 23 for the network's yearly assembly, during which the leadership selection occurred.
