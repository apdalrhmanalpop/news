Saudi Leaders Express Condolences to Egypt's President Over Giza Blaze

King Salman and Crown Prince and Prime Minister Mohammed bin Salman sent messages of condolence to Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi. The Saudi leaders conveyed their profound sympathies regarding the deadly fire incident in the Al-Warraq neighborhood located in the Giza governorate.

The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques and the Crown Prince communicated their sorrow through separate dispatches addressed to the Egyptian head of state, mourning those who lost their lives in the disaster.
