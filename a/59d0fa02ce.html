Millions of Kids Lose Food Stamps Under New Republican Policy

A domestic policy package advanced by Republicans has resulted in two million children losing their food stamp benefits. Proponents of the legislation argued that the reductions were necessary to eliminate fraud and encourage able-bodied adults to secure employment.

Despite the stated goals of the legislative changes, the policy has directly impacted a large population of minors by removing their access to nutritional assistance.
