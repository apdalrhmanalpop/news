Russia and Kazakhstan Reach 96% in National Currency Settlements, Putin States

Nearly all trade transactions between Russia and Kazakhstan are now settled using their domestic currencies, according to statements made by Russian President Vladimir Putin. During a gathering with Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, Putin noted that national currencies account for approximately 96 percent of bilateral exchanges. Details of the discussion were published by the Kremlin's official website.

Additionally, Putin highlighted that the commercial exchange volume between the two nations has surpassed twenty-eight billion US dollars and is steadily nearing the thirty billion US dollar threshold. President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev acknowledged and confirmed these trade figures during their talks.
