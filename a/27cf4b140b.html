Egypt and Qatar Chiefs Confer on Iran Mediation and Financial Ties in Cairo

Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty welcomed Qatari Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani in Cairo on Wednesday. Their meeting focused on bilateral cooperation and broader regional matters.

According to foreign ministry spokesperson Ambassador Nader Zaki, the discussions covered the ongoing crisis involving Iran alongside current mediation efforts, alongside a review of economic relations between the two nations.
