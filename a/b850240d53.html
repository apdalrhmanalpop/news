Saudi Air Defenses Destroy Ballistic Missiles and Drones Targeting Riyadh and Eastern Province

Saudi Arabia's air defense forces successfully intercepted and neutralized a pair of ballistic missiles aimed at the capital city of Riyadh. The hostile projectiles were launched by the Houthi terrorist militia, according to official statements.

In addition to the action over the capital, defense forces also detected and destroyed two unmanned drones operating in the Eastern Province. The military updates were provided by Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki, who serves as the spokesperson for the Coalition to Restore Legitimacy in Yemen.
