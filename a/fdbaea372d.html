Turkish Airlines Launches Africa Fly Fest with Cairo Fares From $240

Turkish Airlines has introduced its Africa Fly Fest promotional campaign, featuring round-trip economy tickets connecting Cairo and Istanbul starting at the price of $240. This announcement coincides with the airline celebrating seventy-five years of operating flights to the Egyptian capital.

The promotional event additionally includes various international destinations accessible through Istanbul. Under this campaign, round-trip journeys originating from Cairo are priced beginning at $430 for Munich and Frankfurt, $445 for Paris, and $450 for other included locations.
