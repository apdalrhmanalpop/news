NDA Falls Short Of Two-Thirds Majority In Lok Sabha Delimitation Vote

The central government's recent push for delimitation faced a setback on April 17, 2026, failing to secure the required majority in the lower house of parliament. During the session, 528 legislators cast their votes on the matter.

The ruling alliance garnered 298 supporting votes, while 230 lawmakers opposed the initiative. Because a two-thirds threshold of 352 votes was necessary for passage, the coalition ultimately fell short by a margin of 54 votes.
