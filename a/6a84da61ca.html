DWP Online Services Taken Offline Due to Cyber Incident

Virtually all digital platforms operated by the Department for Work and Pensions have been suspended following a cyber event.

The disruption has impacted critical online portals, affecting systems designated for pensions, universal credit, and personal independence payment submissions.
