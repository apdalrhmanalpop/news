Coalition Spokesman Blames Houthi Militia for Jazan Refinery Assault

RIYADH: Houthi forces targeted the petroleum facility in Jazan, a military official announced. Maj. Gen. Turki Al-Maliki, who speaks for the coalition backing the legitimate government in Yemen, confirmed the hostile action through official channels.

A strong military retaliation will face any attempts to strike non-combatants and civilian infrastructure, the spokesman warned in a statement published by the Saudi Press Agency.
