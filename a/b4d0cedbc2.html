Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkiye Begin Defense Agreement Talks in Islamabad

Officials from Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkiye commenced discussions in Islamabad on Friday regarding the execution of the Makkah defense agreement, as confirmed to Arab News by a Pakistani Foreign Office representative.

The two-day gathering, scheduled for November 27 to 28, anticipates the participation of the Turkish and Saudi foreign ministers. The talks center on determining practical measures to apply their shared defense commitment.
