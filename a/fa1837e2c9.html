Ministry Announces Progress on Urban Spaces and Housing Targets

The Ministry of Municipalities and Housing reported that urban environment enhancement initiatives have successfully placed 77 percent of inhabitants within an 800-meter proximity of public areas, according to spokesperson Ahmed Al-Dhafiri. These ongoing projects encompass the creation of waterfronts.

Additionally, the ministry outlined its goals for the year 2027, which involve providing a total of 80,000 residential plots and housing units.
