ASEAN Specialists Tour Moscow ATOM Museum to Study Russian Nuclear Sector

Participants in the InteRussia international educational fellowship program "Changing Energy Landscape" recently toured the ATOM Museum located at VDNKh in Moscow. The delegation consisted of professionals hailing from various ASEAN member states, specifically Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Cambodia, and the Philippines.

During the museum visit, the visiting fellows examined an exhibition showcasing the Russian nuclear sector. The tour provided the international specialists with an opportunity to learn about the historical background of the industry.
