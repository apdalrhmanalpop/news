Kingdom Detains Over Nine Thousand Law Breakers in Single Week

Saudi officials took nearly 9,200 individuals into custody for breaking border safety, employment, and residency regulations. The Ministry of Interior stated that these apprehensions occurred throughout the country during a series of coordinated security operations spanning seven days.

The total number of detained offenders comprised 4,797 people who breached residency rules, 2,623 individuals who violated border security protocols, and 1,749 who transgressed labor laws. Additionally, law enforcement officials intercepted 1,373 other individuals during the nationwide sweep.
