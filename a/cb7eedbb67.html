Saudi Arabia Sends Condolences to Egypt Following Fatal Giza Fuel Tanker Blast

Riyadh has shared its profound sympathies with Egypt and the relatives of those who perished in a fuel tanker blast that resulted in thirty-one deaths and twenty-one injuries.

Through a Thursday announcement from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Kingdom expressed its sorrow over the tragedy that occurred along the Ring Road located in the Giza Governorate.
