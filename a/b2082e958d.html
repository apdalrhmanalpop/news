Former UK tech chief suspects cyber attack in DWP system failure

The Department for Work and Pensions has experienced a system outage, leading to questions regarding its potential cause. The UK's former chief technology officer has stated that he holds strong suspicions connecting the technical disruption to a cyber attack.

Authorities have not yet confirmed the underlying reason for the service interruption. Investigations into the incident involving the DWP platform are currently ongoing.
