Pharmacies across England offer children flu vaccine for the first time

Pharmacies throughout England are now providing flu immunizations to thousands of children for the very first time. This initiative aims to reach youngsters who failed to receive their vaccine at school.

The rollout comes following a significant increase in hospital admissions. Over six thousand pharmacy locations are participating in the program to deliver the shots.
