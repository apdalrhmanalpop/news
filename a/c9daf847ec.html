SFDA and SWA Partner to Broaden Institutional Cooperation

The Saudi Food and Drug Authority and the Saudi Water Authority have formally entered into a memorandum of understanding aimed at deepening coordination and partnership across multiple shared domains in Riyadh.

Dr. Hisham Aljadhey, who leads the SFDA as CEO, joined SWA President Eng. Abdullah Al-Abdulkarim to finalize the agreement. The pact focuses on collaborative efforts in research and studies, alongside the utilization of shared capabilities between the two organizations.
