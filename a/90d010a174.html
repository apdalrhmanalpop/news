Iran Engineers Create Robotic Dog for Risky Search and Rescue

Engineers in Iran have created a four-legged robotic dog to aid search-and-rescue missions within dangerous settings, according to a report by Mehr News Agency, which is a TV BRICS partner. This robotic platform is capable of evaluating its environment, locating individuals trapped beneath debris, and transporting emergency supplies.

The machine features human-presence radar, gas sensors, and thermal and night-vision cameras, alongside various other systems.
