California Father Dies in 500-Foot Fall While Aiding Son on Mountain

A California hiking trip ended in tragedy earlier this week when a 44-year-old man plummeted approximately 500 feet to his death on Mount Baden-Powell, located northeast of Los Angeles. The victim, identified as Guillermo Batres, lost his footing as he attempted to assist his teenage son during the excursion.

Nearby witnesses stepped in to aid the young man following the accident. These two individuals successfully rescued the teenager and contacted emergency services by dialing 911.
