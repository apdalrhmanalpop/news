Upcoming aircraft carrier honors Pearl Harbor sailor Doris Miller

Next week marks the start of construction for a brand-new aircraft carrier named after Doris Miller. During the attack on Pearl Harbor, the sailor worked to move injured individuals into safe locations and operated a machine gun against enemy aircraft from Japan.

The upcoming vessel will ensure that the legacy of the heroic sailor continues to be remembered.
