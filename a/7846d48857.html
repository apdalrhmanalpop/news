Holiday Storm Disrupts Travel and Cuts Power to Thousands on East Coast

A powerful storm system impacted the East Coast over the Thanksgiving holiday, bringing severe winds and heavy precipitation to regions stretching between Virginia and New York. The adverse weather severely hampered transportation networks during one of the busiest travel periods of the year.

The severe conditions knocked out electricity for approximately five hundred thousand customers across the affected area. Forecasts indicate that the weather is slated to experience a slow recovery starting on Friday.
