Netanyahu Proposes Rotation Deal Putting Eisenkot First

Israeli caretaker leader Benjamin Netanyahu expressed openness on Saturday to a power-sharing agreement that would allow opposition politician Gadi Eisenkot to assume the premiership first.

Netanyahu stated that severe dangers facing the nation warrant setting aside the traditional practice where the largest political faction automatically heads the administration.
