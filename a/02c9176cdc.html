Makkah Defense Alliance Accelerates Efforts to Protect Saudi Security

The Political and Defense Strategic Committee of the Makkah Defense Alliance has committed to speeding up collaborative initiatives aimed at backing Saudi Arabia's security protection. During a gathering in Islamabad, representatives from Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Türkiye convened for the committee's second session.

Officials renewed their dedication to advancing these shared actions in alignment with the collective defense guidelines outlined in the founding agreement of the alliance.
