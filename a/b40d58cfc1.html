Norway's Norse Atlantic Declares Bankruptcy Amid Fuel Price Surge

A budget carrier established just a few years back has filed for bankruptcy after failing to achieve profitability. The low-cost airline was ultimately pushed into insolvency by soaring aviation fuel expenses triggered by the conflict in Iran.

Norway-based Norse Atlantic had experienced ongoing difficulties making money before the dramatic escalation in fuel costs proved too much for the business to survive.
