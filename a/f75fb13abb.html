Motorists Evading Tolls Face Hefty Penalties Beyond Standard Fee

Drivers attempting to bypass toll charges by concealing their vehicle's license plates could face substantial financial consequences. Authorities have established a fine system where motorists caught evading fees will be required to cover a penalty significantly higher than the standard rate.

For instance, an individual trying to avoid a standard one-hundred-rupee toll payment will ultimately face a total charge of six hundred rupees. This includes the original fee plus an additional five-hundred-rupee penalty applied on top of the initial amount.
