Planning Board Schedules Final Meeting on Trump Washington Projects

The National Capital Planning Commission has scheduled an eleventh-hour session to evaluate construction initiatives backed by President Trump. This upcoming gathering represents a final opportunity for the current administration to secure approvals for favored developments before a shift in congressional leadership.

Control over the planning agency is set to transition away from the president when Democrats assume power in Congress this coming January. Consequently, this immediate review session could grant definitive clearance to several high-priority projects associated with the outgoing administration.
